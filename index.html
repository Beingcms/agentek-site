A few good questions

Keep the decision
grounded.

Do we need our own model?

Not necessarily. An existing model with good instructions or access to approved knowledge may solve the task. Owning or fine-tuning weights is useful only when the benefits justify the extra preparation, compute and operation.

What can ModelFit do today?

ModelFit is a guided private beta. The validated prototype compares small 135-million and 360-million parameter text models, with a training recipe for the 135-million parameter model. Frontier-provider entries currently show price references, not completed live comparisons. Larger models and additional tasks require their own validation. Prototype results are not customer proof.

How would we teach it?

Your experts provide examples and review the desired answers. We prepare the data and reserve separate test examples. Fine-tuning changes a model’s learned behaviour; connecting approved knowledge lets it use current information. We choose the approach to fit the task. Synthetic examples need review and do not replace representative business data.

Where would our model run?

We scope an approved model API, dedicated cloud infrastructure or customer hardware according to the model and your constraints. Open weights still need compute and support. Current prototype operation depends on an operator-run local machine; always-on hosting, GPU capacity and customer hardware are not yet qualified services.

What does control mean in practice?

Agree data access, model licences, deployment location, acceptance rules and who approves releases. A fine-tuned adapter still needs its compatible base model. Ownership and permitted use of data, software and model artifacts are set out in the engagement terms, not assumed from the word “open”.

Can I open the studio now?

The current workspace is invitation-only and requires authorised access. Open your invited workspace if you already have access, or discuss a pilot below. There is no public self-service signup at this stage.
