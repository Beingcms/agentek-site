AgenTek Data Preparation

Your data doesn’t have
to arrive ready.

Have documents and records, but no training dataset? We help turn permitted source material into reviewed examples and a trustworthy test set. It is a managed service, scoped with your team before data is transferred.

01 / Understand

Find what matters.

Map the task, available sources, permissions and missing examples. Agree what a good answer looks like and which errors must block release.

02 / Clean

Keep what is needed.

Standardise formats, remove duplicates and flag incomplete or conflicting records. Remove or mask unnecessary personal and sensitive details, with your data owner’s review.

03 / Review

Teach the right behaviour.

Prepare labels and example answers for your subject experts to approve. Include edge cases and when the model should decline. Synthetic variations stay identified and reviewed.

04 / Test

Keep the exam separate.

Group related examples, reserve independent tests and check for overlap with training data. Record source history, review decisions and quality gaps before an experiment starts.

A usable handover.

You receive an agreed source inventory, a prepared example pack, a labelling guide, a data-quality report and separate training and test sets. Remaining gaps stay visible.

Preparation effort and review responsibilities are agreed up front. Larger cleaning or labelling projects are scoped separately from evaluation, training and hosting. This is expert-assisted preparation, not automatic anonymisation or a guarantee of model accuracy.

Tokens are the chunks of text providers bill for. Input includes instructions and document text. Billed output must include any charged reasoning tokens as well as the visible answer. This example assumes one successful call per document, standard uncached text pricing, no tools, no retries and no batch discounts.

API usage = documents × (input tokens × input rate + billed output tokens × output rate) ÷ 1,000,000. Server rental = $0.80 × hours × servers. Idle time still costs money. Fewer hours assume scheduled work and may mean waiting for the server to start; more traffic may need more servers.

For your technical team: GPUs, vLLM and Ollama

A GPU is a processor suited to model training and serving. Training compute is booked for an agreed experiment; a serving endpoint needs separate capacity for live requests. vLLM is an option for serving compatible models on suitable GPUs. Ollama or llama.cpp can suit supported local formats. The model, adapter, conversion and hardware combination must be tested together; a Hugging Face download alone does not make every model compatible.

A few good questions

Keep the decision
grounded.

Do we need our own model?

Not necessarily. An existing model with good instructions or access to approved knowledge may solve the task. Owning or fine-tuning weights is useful only when the benefits justify the extra preparation, compute and operation.

What can ModelFit do today?

ModelFit is a guided private beta. The validated prototype compares small 135-million and 360-million parameter text models, with a training recipe for the 135-million parameter model. Frontier-provider entries currently show price references, not completed live comparisons. Five synthetic task demonstrations show the training and checking workflow, with actual answers and visible failures. Larger models and customer tasks require their own validation. Inspect the experiments. Prototype results are not customer proof.

How would we teach it?

Your experts provide examples and review the desired answers. We prepare the data and reserve separate test examples. Fine-tuning changes a model’s learned behaviour; connecting approved knowledge lets it use current information. We choose the approach to fit the task. Synthetic examples need review and do not replace representative business data.

Does the model learn from every interaction?

No. The private-beta correction workflow starts with a completed Playground answer. An owner reviews each correction before exporting the evidence. It does not automatically collect live-service conversations, train on feedback or change the model. Examples already used for testing need explicit exclusions; they cannot become fresh final tests.

Is retraining included in managed support?

Periodic correction review can be included within the agreed support hours. Data preparation, further evaluation, retraining and release changes are scoped separately. Any training has an agreed compute limit. A correction can guide the next experiment, but it does not guarantee a better model.

Where would our model run?

Our preferred managed route is a qualified endpoint in an account your business controls, with infrastructure billed directly to you and AgenTek charging separately for delivery and operation. Customer-hardware handover and existing model APIs are alternatives. Production capacity and support are agreed after the pilot. See the three hosting options.

What does control mean in practice?

Agree data access, model licences, deployment location, acceptance rules and who approves releases. A fine-tuned adapter still needs its compatible base model. Ownership and permitted use of data, software and model artifacts are set out in the engagement terms, not assumed from the word “open”.

Can I open the studio now?

The current workspace is invitation-only and requires authorised access. See Studio sign-in and access details if you already have access, or discuss a pilot below. There is no public self-service signup at this stage.
