For your technical team

Your infrastructure.
Your model package.

Receive the supported trained adaptation, base-model requirements, test evidence and running instructions. Your team runs it on compatible, tested hardware.

Handover is scoped. A model package is not a standalone app. An SDK, a developer toolkit, can simplify integration later if needed.

The Studio hosts the task, examples and evidence. Separate compute trains the model and answers live requests. A single website address does not mean all three run on the same server. See hosting responsibilities and costs →

Can we use our company sign-in?

Company single sign-on means using your existing workplace identity. It is part of the proposed production deployment, not an active feature of this preview. We agree identity provider, team permissions and data boundaries during qualification.
