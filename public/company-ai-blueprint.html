What you receive

Six deliverables.
One coherent route.

A planning package built around your workflow, with assumptions and decisions visible to both business and technical teams.

01

Workflow opportunity brief

The problem, users, current effort, exceptions, desired outcomes and how value would be measured.

02

Company context map

The knowledge, data, terminology, systems, permissions and human decisions the workflow depends on.

03

Evaluation and control plan

Representative scenarios, expected behaviour, critical failures, escalation rules and proposed release thresholds.

04

Solution architecture

The proposed combination of instructions, knowledge retrieval, model adaptation, workflow logic and integrations.

05

Economics and options

Build and buy choices, model and hosting options, delivery assumptions, recurring costs and trade-offs.

06

Implementation roadmap

A sequenced plan with ownership, dependencies, milestones and evidence needed for the next investment decision.

The Blueprint defines the evaluation plan. It does not by itself deliver a trained model, completed production implementation or proof of business accuracy. Those activities need an agreed delivery scope.
